# Travelling waves

Open project.oes.json in Studio and press **▶ Try it** (F5), or open it in the standalone Player.

Press **▶ Try it** (F5) in Studio, or open it in the Player. Two real local Theora movies play, with a scene transition at six seconds. The twelve-second timeline loops. Next film advances manually.

**Installation.** Single display. FFmpeg is only needed to encode new video assets.
