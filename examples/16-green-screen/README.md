# Step into another world — green-screen photo booth

Visitors stand in front of a green cloth. A webcam films them, the green is replaced live by a
background video or picture, and they can take a photo or a short video and download it on their
phone by scanning a QR code (local network only; files are deleted after 30 minutes).

1. Open this exhibit in Studio and go to **Green screen**.
2. Choose the local network under **Who can download**, then press **Start booth**. The booth opens
   in the browser on this computer; allow the camera when asked.
3. Press **Ctrl+Shift+S** on the booth for setup: pick the camera, press **Detect from edges** with
   nobody in front of the cloth, then fine-tune until **Show matte** shows people white and the
   cloth black. **Save**, and in Studio use **Save booth settings into this exhibit**.

The three backgrounds (*Deep space*, *Under the sea*, *Sunset hills*) are small drawn samples.
Replace them with your own WebM/MP4 videos or PNG/JPEG pictures under **Green screen →
Backgrounds**. The full guide is [docs/GREEN-SCREEN.md](../../docs/GREEN-SCREEN.md).
